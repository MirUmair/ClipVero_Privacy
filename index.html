Welcome to ClipVero ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to transparency regarding how our mobile application interacts with your device and data.

Core Commitment: ClipVero operates as an offline-first video editor. We have no external servers collecting or storing your personal videos, photos, or voice recordings.

1. On-Device Media Processing

When you edit videos, trim clips, add stickers, adjust audio, or export files using ClipVero, all calculations and file renderings are executed entirely on your Android device's hardware using native AndroidX Media3 technologies.

Your source media and rendered video files remain stored exclusively on your device's local memory or external storage.

2. Android Permissions and Usage

To enable video editing features, ClipVero requests specific operating system permissions. Each permission is requested strictly for core functionality:

  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO & READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
    Gallery Access: Allows you to pick your personal video clips and images to import them onto the timeline.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO & RECORD_AUDIO
    Audio & Microphone: Allows you to select background soundtracks from your library or record optional voiceover audio. Recordings are saved strictly onto your local storage.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE & WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
    Storage Access (Android 12 and below): Required on legacy Android versions to read media files and save rendered videos into your Movies/Gallery folder.
  • VIBRATE
    Haptics: Provides gentle haptic vibrations when snapping clips or dragging stickers on the preview canvas.
  • INTERNET
    Network Connectivity: Used for opening external share intents and standard system network checks. No user videos or photos are transmitted over the internet.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

ClipVero does not transmit your personal data to external third parties. When you tap the Share button on an exported video, the app invokes the standard Android Sharesheet. The file is shared solely with the specific application you select (such as Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, or YouTube).

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

ClipVero is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because our app operates locally and does not collect personal data, we adhere to COPPA and GDPR-K guidelines.

5. Data Security

Since your files are saved and processed locally on your phone, security is maintained by your Android operating system's security sandbox. We recommend keeping your Android device updated with the latest security patches.
